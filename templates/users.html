🎂 Birthday celebration coordinators (optional)

Tick the people who organise birthday celebrations (for example the welfare or social committee). They get two extra emails: one the day before a birthday and one list on the first working day of each month. They only hear about people who chose to share their birthday with them, and never about their own. If nobody is ticked, those two emails simply aren't sent; the bell, the Monday line, the home card and the printable list all still work.
