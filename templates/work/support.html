How these counts are defined

Targets before today are overdue; due today is separate. Missing targets are never treated as overdue. A review wait is elapsed calendar days since its latest recorded submission or routing, not a promised service time.

Outcomes needing support have at least one recorded signal: missing or unavailable lead, unassigned or unavailable contribution owner, waiting contribution, overdue contribution or work-item target, a work-item target within 7 calendar days including today, or an overdue required official record. These are prompts for coordination, not a prediction or staff performance score. Missing dates alone do not create a risk flag.

Unit filtering uses the work item’s concerned unit, including all its contributions. Formal reviews count each linked official document once. Reference links alone do not impose a release obligation. Review age is unknown when its timestamp is missing or in the future.
