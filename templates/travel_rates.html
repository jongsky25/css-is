How a day's DTE is shared (VI.A.2.i)

  • Arrival day and each day of official business: 100%. Lodging 50%, meals 30%, incidentals 20%.
  • Departure day, when you left the station the day before you arrived: 50%. Meals 30%, incidentals 20%.
  • The Php 50 subsistence allowance paid through payroll comes off the meals of a working day, not a weekend or holiday.
  • Hotel and meals provided (live-in): incidentals of 20% for the day before and the day after.
  • No double payment (VI.A.2.k): a part that the government or someone else paid for is not allowed.
  • Lodging above its share (VI.A.2.j) can be claimed by up to 100% more, with the agency head's certification and the hotel bill and official receipts. The prepare page does not work this out; the extra goes to the Admin Unit.

Public fare beyond 50 km (Annex B)

When no local rate per passenger exists, the fare from the point of disembarkation to the place of assignment follows the LTFRB ordinary bus fare guide for Metro Manila. First 5 km Php 10.00, then Php 1.85 for each km, to the nearest 25 centavos. Seniors, PWDs and students pay 20% less (Php 8.00, then Php 1.48 for each km). A part of a kilometre counts as a whole one; past 60 km the same method continues.
