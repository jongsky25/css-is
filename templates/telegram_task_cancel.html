Cancel an unfinished task

Telegram connection and permissions · Review tasks and dependencies

Start with /task_cancel in your linked private chat. The concerned officer for the task's contributing unit must have the source cancellation authority. Work-lead, task-owner or Telegram consent alone grants no such authority.

Cancellation removes this task from outstanding-contribution checks. This includes required contributions. Cancellation does not accept output; dependent tasks awaiting this task's acceptance remain blocked. Work completion and official document decisions still require their separate source workflows.

Proposed, To do, In progress and For review tasks may be cancelled. Owner fields, previous submission, expected output, deadline and history are retained. Cancelled tasks cannot use task reopening. Review submissions and dependencies in Office Connect first; stored submission contents stay there. Normal membership rules apply, so an owner may lose work access without another source right.

Done/cancelled tasks, completed work and active formal consolidation review amendments stay in Office Connect. This action does not delete a task, cancel a whole work case or alter an official document.
