Follow or unfollow a discussion

Telegram connection and permissions · Open source discussions

Start /discussion_follow in your linked private chat. Choose the exact work and optional task, then review your own current follow and proposed change in Telegram before confirming.

A work-wide follow covers work-level and all task discussions. A task-specific follow covers only that task. Unfollowing a task does not remove a work-wide follow; unfollowing work does not remove task-specific follows. Other accounts are never changed.

Future native follower notices require current source access. Existing notices/read status, direct mentions/replies, task progress and approvals stay unchanged. This does not enable Telegram/email delivery or change existing delivery preferences.
