{# What a meal request's status means to the person who made it (spot feedback #197): there is no approval step. #}
How do I know my request went through?

Meal requests are not approved. The Administrative Section orders the meals and records delivery, so the status of each meal is the answer:

  • Awaiting order — your request was received and has not been ordered yet.
  • Ordered — the Administrative Section has placed the order with the supplier. This is your confirmation.
  • Delivered / Not delivered — recorded on or after the activity date.
  • Cancelled / Withdrawn — the meal or the whole request will not be served.

Nothing is sent to you when the status changes, so open this page to check. When a meal is marked, the date it was marked is shown beside it.
