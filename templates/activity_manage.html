Certificate of Appearance

When enabled, a participant who has completed all of this activity's feedback can download an auto-generated DOH Certificate of Appearance as a print-ready PDF. The participant's name, the activity title, date and venue are filled in automatically; the signatory's name, title and signature image come from the Directory. Only the bureau's signing authorities — Section Chiefs, Division Chiefs and the Director — can be chosen as the signatory.

Don't see a signature? Add one on the person's Directory record.
