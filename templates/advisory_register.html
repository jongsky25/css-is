How advisories work
  1. Start: press New advisory and say what it is for: an activity, a new activity, or a general notice.
  2. Write it: you and your section edit it in Google Slides.
  3. Review: your Division Chief, then the Director, approve it or send it back with comments.
  4. Issue: it is emailed to the people you chose, and you can share it to group chats.
