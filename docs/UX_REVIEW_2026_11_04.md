## Recommended order

1. **Make the requested reporting window complete.** `app.py` caps all insights reads at 5,000 newest events. Aggregate over the full date range in storage, with separately bounded samples and explicit requested/observed dates. Add release/date filters and test-traffic segmentation. Merely increasing the cap is a temporary measure.
2. **Improve wellness time selection and validation.** `/wellness/apply`: 13 dead clicks from eight users around the exact-minute control; 13 HTTP 400 responses, three client form errors, 14 submit events and two abandon events. The current template has a separate custom-time radio, label and time input; its help explicitly says typed times are checked on submission. Make selecting an exact time select that option clearly, explain unavailable slots on touch, validate early using consistent rules, and preserve entered fields. The click signal and errors are not proven causally connected.
3. **Investigate filing reliability before visual redesign.** `/work-arrangement/file`: three HTTP 500s, seven HTTP 400s and six form errors; wellness completion: one HTTP 500. Correlate with version/time and existing fixes, then reproduce with synthetic fixtures. Count successful server outcomes separately from submit attempts. Do not weaken valid permission/validation rules in response to 400/403 counts.
4. **Improve check-in discovery.** Six of ten recorded searches were empty, including check-in spelling variants and wellness-entitlement questions. Normalize hyphens and synonyms, expose a direct check-in result with its current availability, and route question-style input to the appropriate existing help. This is a small sample, not a general search failure rate.
5. **Clarify home-page actions.** Companion container: 37 dead clicks from eight users; broader header: 36 from four; companion controls: 29 rapid taps from 11. Make actionable targets and their feedback clear. Do not make the entire mixed-control header clickable. Rapid companion taps can be intentional play. Your to-do heading also has 12 dead clicks from six users; consider an explicit view-all action.
6. **Measure calendar slow tails.** Event details: 59 timing samples, median load 785 ms, p90 7,409 ms; median TTFB 617 ms. Calendar itself: 216 samples, median 622 ms, p90 3,151 ms. Split by device/network/version and asset versus server timing before choosing an optimization. The details sheet also records ten dead clicks from seven users, but container clicks alone do not establish a broken control.
