# Proposed advisory memo instructions

Prepared to match the Office Connect workflow. This is replacement text for review;
the external memo has not been edited.

1. Prepare an advisory from its Office Connect activity. Select the owning division,
   internal users, external recipients, or both. Save the draft and check the saved PDF.
   The advisory number is reserved on first save; the actual issue date is recorded later.
2. Submit the saved revision to the owning Division Chief. The Chief may comment,
   return it with required feedback, or endorse it to the Director. The Director may
   comment, return it or approve that exact revision. Current formal OIC appointments
   apply. The preparer, Chief reviewer and Director approver must be different people.
3. Respond to feedback in the advisory discussion. Each comment and reply retains its
   author, time and target revision. Reviewers resolve or reopen concerns. Revised
   content or audience returns through Chief review and Director approval. An unchanged
   clarification remains at the current stage. No decision is made by replying to email.
4. After approval, the initiator or an explicitly assigned Records officer reviews the
   approved PDF and final recipient list, acknowledges missing email addresses, and
   confirms issuance. No separate signature step is required. Approval alone does not
   issue, email or publish the advisory.
5. Office Connect records an immutable issued copy, actual issue date and recipient
   snapshot. Active eligible internal users are resolved at issuance. Internal/external
   duplicate addresses receive one individual email with the approved PDF; recipient
   addresses are not disclosed to one another. External recipients need no login to
   read the attachment. Internal recipients can also use Issued advisories for you.
6. Follow delivery separately from issuance. Retry only a confirmed failed/no-effect
   attempt. For uncertain outcomes, check the mail service and record evidence before
   any retry. Accepted email is not a read receipt. Cancellation cannot recall delivered mail.
7. Public publication is a separate explicit action for an external/both-audience issue.
   Internal circulation does not make an advisory public. Replacement revisions repeat
   review and approval, retain old artifacts and actions, and link the superseded issue.
8. Use the Office Connect advisory register and its status, month, division and audience
   filters for monitoring. Office Connect holds the register, discussion, decisions,
   issued copies and delivery history. No separate Sheet workflow or synchronization
   is required.
