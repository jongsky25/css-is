## Why this file exists

This is the durable, repository-held description of the bureau's units,
mandates and functions. It is meant to be reused for:

- seeding and checking the Office Connect directory tree (`org_unit`);
- approval/escalation routing (which Director oversees which unit);
- document intake routing rules (which section handles a topic);
- unit mailboxes and division/section filters;
- staff-facing guidance and verified answers ("who handles LIPH?").

The machine-readable companion is [`blhsd_interim_structure_2026.json`](blhsd_interim_structure_2026.json).
The impact assessment is [ORG_RESTRUCTURE_ASSESSMENT.md](../ORG_RESTRUCTURE_ASSESSMENT.md).

**Privacy:** this repository is public. Staff names, employment status of
named persons and allowance authorizations are intentionally omitted here.
Seat holders belong in the application directory (database), entered by an
administrator, not in tracked files. Seats are described by title only.

## Shape of the new structure

```
BLHSD (including Technical Office functions)
├── Office of the Director IV ── oversees Divisions 1, 2, 3
│   ├── Division 1  HSIDD   — Sections 1, 2
│   ├── Division 2  HSGPAD  — Sections 3, 4
│   └── Division 3  LHSIID  — Sections 5, 6
├── Office of the Director III ── oversees Divisions 4, 5 and the MSU
│   ├── Division 4  LHPFD   — Sections 7, 8
│   ├── Division 5  LHSSD*  — Sections 9, 10
│   └── Management Support Unit (MSU)
└── Administrative Unit (AU) — supports both Directors
```

\* The order gives no acronym for Division 5 ("Local Health Systems Support
Division"); the office confirmed `LHSSD` (November 23, 2026).

Key differences from the structure currently modelled in Office Connect
(2 divisions — HSDD and HSMED — with 5 sections, an Administrative section and
one Office of the Director):

- **Two Directors with split oversight.** Director IV (overall leadership;
  Divisions 1–3) and Director III (CHD/LGU operationalization; Divisions 4–5
  and MSU). The AU supports both.
- **Five divisions, ten numbered sections.** Topics move between units (for
  example LIPH/AOP and financing leave the old development division for
  Division 4; governance/equity and LGU performance combine in Division 2).
- **New non-division units:** Management Support Unit (under Director III) and
  Administrative Unit (lead: Administrative Officer IV).
- **Each division has a designated division admin** (an ADAS/NA staff member),
  and several staff hold more than one assignment (e.g. one person is support
  staff in a Director's office, a member of the AU, and a division admin).
- **Officers-in-Charge** lead Divisions 1, 3 and 4.
- **Transition rule:** for two weeks from effectivity existing work arrangements
  may continue. Commitments under approved DPCRs/IPCRs stay with the originally
  responsible staff; reassignment needs justification and approval of both
  Directors, as applicable.
- **Flagship JO/COS staff** continue duties for UNA Para sa Kalusugan, DOH
  Wellness Clinics (DWC) program management and Integrated Field Visits
  Supportive Supervision (IFVSS) at priority sites.

## A. Office of the Director

### Office of the Director IV
Seat: Director IV (OIC-Director IV, "BLHSD including TO"). Support: Senior
Health Program Officer (JO), Administrative Assistant III, Administrative Aide IV
(driver).

1. Provide overall strategic leadership in strengthening and integrating local
   health systems through alignment of DOH-wide policies, programs and
   initiatives with UHC priorities.
2. Ensure alignment and harmonization of DOH programs for coordinated
   implementation through CHDs and LGUs.
3. Establish strategic directions and partnerships to strengthen local health
   systems and accelerate UHC implementation.
4. Exercise direct management oversight over **Divisions 1, 2 and 3**.

### Office of the Director III
Seat: Director III. Support: Administrative Assistant III, Nursing Attendant II (JO).

1. Coordinate the operationalization of policies, programs and initiatives on
   local health systems development at CHD and LGU levels.
2. Oversee coordinated technical assistance to CHDs and LGUs in implementing
   UNA for Health and UHC priorities.
3. Monitor implementation issues and facilitate inter-office coordination to
   address gaps and improve local health systems performance.
4. Exercise direct management oversight over **Divisions 4, 5 and the MSU**.

### Management Support Unit (MSU)
Lead: Chief Administrative Officer. Members: AO II (JO), two NA II (JO).

1. Under the direct management oversight of the Director III.
2. Management and technical support to Directors III and IV in strategic
   planning, policy coordination, performance monitoring and decision-making.
3. Coordinate and track implementation of directives, priority initiatives and
   commitments across BLHSD divisions; facilitate inter-office coordination.
4. Prepare management briefs, executive reports and strategic analyses.

### Administrative Unit (AU)
Lead: Administrative Officer IV. Members: ADAS III ×5 (two JO), AA IV,
Senior Administrative Assistant II, NA II (JO) ×2. Several members also serve
as Director-office support or division admins.

1. Administrative and logistical support to Directors III and IV: personnel
   administration, records management, communications, scheduling and office
   operations.
2. Timely processing of financial, procurement, property, supply and other
   administrative requirements under government rules.
3. Efficient management of office resources, administrative records and
   logistics for meetings, official activities and other BLHSD operations.
4. Human resource policies: leaves, hiring/recruitment, resignation and
   retirement, contract renewal, and maintenance of 201 files.

## B. Division 1 — Health Systems Integration and Development Division (HSIDD)
Oversight: Director IV. Chief: OIC (Medical Officer IV). Division admin: ADAS III.

Mandate:
- Frameworks, policies, standards and tools; technical guidance for functional
  Province-/City-wide Health Systems (P/CWHS), Health Care Provider Networks
  (HCPN) and Primary Care Provider Networks (PCPN).
- Institutionalizing primary health care: service delivery models, empanelment,
  YAKAP, GAMOT, other primary care benefit packages; BHW capacity and support.
- Monitoring, assessment and improvement of LHS integration and primary care:
  LHS Maturity Level, HCPN/PCPN functionality, service delivery models, UHC
  grants to LGUs.

**Section 1 — Local Health Systems Integration Section.** Head: SHPO.
Mailboxes: healthsystemsblhsd@doh.gov.ph, hcpn@doh.gov.ph.
1. Policies, frameworks, standards and tools for managerial and technical
   integration of the P/CWHS, with relevant DOH bureaus/services.
2. Lead monitoring and updating of LHS ML and provision of UHC grants to LGUs.
3. Integrated referral pathways and service delivery models across levels of
   care in P/CWHS.
4. Policies/tools for private sector engagement in the P/CWHS.
5. Technical assistance in establishing, contracting and sustaining HCPNs/PCPNs.
6. Monitor functionality and promote working models of HCPN and/or P/CWHS.

**Section 2 — Primary Care Section.** Head: Medical Officer III (MedPool).
Mailbox: blhsdpurokalusugan@doh.gov.ph.
1. Policies, standards and tools for primary care service delivery.
2. Strategies and guidance on BHW capacity, roles and support mechanisms as
   members of primary care teams.
3. Institutionalization of primary care mechanisms and programs: empanelment,
   YAKAP, GAMOT and other primary care benefit packages.
4. Integration of primary care services into local health systems.

## C. Division 2 — Health Systems Governance and Performance Accountability Division (HSGPAD)
Oversight: Director IV. Chief: Medical Officer V. Division admin: ADAS III.

Mandate:
- Local health governance, institutional arrangements and equitable access,
  particularly for unserved/underserved communities including ICCs/IPs.
- Policies and guidelines to assess and monitor LHS performance.
- Identification, documentation and replication of good practices.
- Awarding and recognition programs for excellence in LHS development.

**Section 3 — Governance and Equity Section.** Head: CHPO.
Mailbox: healthequity@doh.gov.ph.
1. Local health governance and accountability, including Local Health Board
   functionality.
2. Equitable, culturally sensitive services in unserved/underserved
   communities including ICCs/IPs, and their participation.
3. Technical assistance on local health governance and equitable LHS development.
4. Monitoring and evaluation of those policies and initiatives.

**Section 4 — Performance Accountability Section.** Head: Medical Officer IV.
Mailbox: lguhsc@doh.gov.ph.
1. Indicators and tools to track LHS performance (e.g. LGU Health Scorecard,
   Seal of Good Local Governance).
2. Consolidation of LGU information nationwide (socio-demographic, political,
   economic, environmental, etc.).
3. Identification, documentation and replication of good practices.
4. Awarding and recognition guidelines for LHS excellence.

## D. Division 3 — Local Health Systems Intelligence and Innovation Division (LHSIID)
Oversight: Director IV. Chief: OIC (CHPO). Division admin: ADAS III (JO).
The order leaves the division mandate blank.

**Section 5 — Data Systems, Analytics and Innovation Section.** Head: SHPO.
No mailbox listed.
1. Bureau-level corporate and sectoral information systems, digital platforms
   and data infrastructure. *(Office Connect itself falls under this mandate.)*
2. Integrated LHS databases, LGU profiling, performance dashboards and analytics.
3. Data integration, interoperability, quality assurance, governance and secure
   information sharing.
4. Digital transformation and innovation: technology-enabled solutions and
   automated processes.

**Section 6 — Knowledge Management and Capacity Development Section.**
Head: SHPO. Mailbox: blhsdleadgov@doh.gov.ph.
1. Bureau-wide knowledge management systems.
2. Knowledge exchange and learning: research, evidence synthesis, communities
   of practice, peer learning across DOH offices, CHDs, DOH Representatives, LGUs.
3. Capacity development programs for DOH Representatives, CHDs, LGUs.
4. Operational policies and tools for DOH Representatives' roles and performance.

## E. Division 4 — Local Health Planning and Financing Division (LHPFD)
Oversight: Director III. Chief: OIC (CHPO). Division admin: ADAS III (JO).

Mandate:
- Local health planning, investment programming and resource allocation,
  including LIPH and AOP.
- Local health financing and financial integration: Special Health Fund (SHF),
  contracting for population-based services, LGU health expenditure tracking.
- Resource requirements, financing gaps and fiscal needs; resource mobilization;
  consolidation of the DOH Menu of Assistance to LGUs.

**Section 7 — Local Health Planning Section.** Head: SHPO.
Mailbox: blhsdliph@doh.gov.ph.
1. Policies and tools on local health planning and investment programming (LIPH, AOP).
2. Consolidate, analyze and cascade the national DOH Menu of Assistance to LGUs.
3. Analyze LGU TA needs in LIPH/AOP via P/CDOHOs; match TA and grants from DOH
   bureaus/services.

**Section 8 — Local Health Financing Section.** Head: SHPO.
Mailbox: blhsdlhf@doh.gov.ph.
1. Local health financing and financial integration: SHF operationalization and
   monitoring; contracting for population-based services (e.g. Terms of Partnership).
2. Institutionalize LGU health expenditure tracking.
3. Assess LGU resource requirements, financing gaps and fiscal needs; resource
   mobilization, strategic purchasing and UHC alignment.

## F. Division 5 — Local Health Systems Support Division
Oversight: Director III. Chief: CHPO. Division admin: NA II (JO).

Mandate:
1. Assess and strengthen CHD organizational structures and institutional capacities.
2. Institutional arrangements, coordination mechanisms and operational
   governance enabling CHDs and LGUs in UHC implementation.
3. Regional coordination and integrated field visits supportive supervision,
   including regional health governance mechanisms.

**Section 9 — Institutional Supportive Supervision and Coordination Section.**
Head: Medical Officer IV (detailed). No mailbox listed.
1. Coordination/support mechanisms for strategic initiatives (e.g. DOH Wellness
   Clinics); alignment and issue resolution.
2. Integrated field visits supportive supervision (IFVSS) to identify needs,
   challenges and TA requirements of CHDs and LGUs.
3. Regional health initiatives and governance mechanisms: Regional Health
   Councils, institutional recognition, inter-agency initiatives.

**Section 10 — Local Optimization and Implementation Section.**
Head: Medical Officer III (detailed). No mailbox listed.
1. Optimization, structures and mechanisms of regional counterparts and
   Provincial/City DOH Offices (P/CDOHOs).
2. National–regional implementation governance to resolve TA needs of CHDs,
   P/CDOHOs and LGUs.
3. Convergence of DOH programs and regional counterparts.
4. Standardization of CHD and P/CDOHO operating budget for mandatory expenses.
