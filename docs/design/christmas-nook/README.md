# Festive companion room — candidate 9.97

The companion's plain green stage becomes a decorated Christmas room: garlands,
cranberry bows, warm capiz windows, stockings, gifts and a woven rug. This
complements the parol header without repeating its greeting or central lanterns.
The existing animated companion, saved outfit, sound controls and edit link remain
live. No personal or production records were used.

Two original AI-generated backgrounds were made from the approved design study
using OpenAI image generation. WebP encoding uses quality 84. The phone asset is
313,388 bytes; the panoramic asset is 172,440 bytes. The panorama has black
letterboxing outside the central room band, clipped by its CSS container. Neither
asset includes a baked-in companion or interface. Artwork is decorative, static,
and excluded in high contrast, forced colours and print; the artwork opt-out
continues to apply.

The room uses a portrait-friendly composition below 700px and the panorama above
that breakpoint. Sound controls sit below the room; the companion's size still
reflects its saved growth stage.
